As they entered the temple, Casey felt the hairs on the back of his neck go up. This was the kind of place where someone could get caught by booby traps, Indiana Jones style. However, he wasn’t going to let his fear show in front of his father. He was determined to keep going, even though all his senses told him to get out as fast as he could.

“All right, let’s split up and explore the temple. If someone is in trouble or gets lost, just give a shout. Casey, Roberto, and I will go down this side passage; the rest of you keep exploring this corridor,” James said.

They started down the narrow side passage, which seemed to get increasingly narrower and sloped downward as they went on. Before long, they were all practically sliding down the rock face. Finally, they all crumpled to a stop at the bottom.

“I don’t think we’re going to be able to climb back up that,” said Casey worriedly.

“Probably not,” agreed James. “Let’s all split up and look for a way out. It looks like we’ve come out in a fairly large room. Casey, Roberto, you guys go along the walls, and I’ll go look in the middle.”

As Casey’s eyes adjusted to the darkness, he realized that James was right. They were in a huge circular room. He found his flashlight and took it out. When he snapped it on, he saw many markings on the walls. They depicted gruesome scenes of ancient people sacrificing hearts to their gods. He quickly hurried on, only to come to a rock face. This didn’t look like part of the temple. It was untouched natural rock. He shined his flashlight all around and realized that it stretched to occupy this whole side of the room.

“Maybe this is where the temple ends,” he said quietly to himself. He put his hand to the wall of stone. Strangely, it felt slightly warm. He pushed harder, and suddenly it gave way beneath his palms. He staggered forward from the force of his push. Then, reacting too slowly, he hit his head and his world went black.
