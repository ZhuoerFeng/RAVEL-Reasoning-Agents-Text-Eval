“What in the world?!” exclaimed someone in the crew. Everyone began to yell and point. 

“Everyone calm down,” James shouted over the commotion. “Let’s just go down to the temple and set up camp.” 

Casey knew that this was not on their agenda. They weren’t expecting to find a temple in the middle of an unmapped valley, yet there it stood. “Dad, the dig site isn’t for another few kilometers, so why are we stopping now?” 

“Casey, I think this is more important than a dig site, don’t you?” James said. 

“I guess,” Casey agreed. 

They slowly made their way through the foliage toward the temple. After seemingly endless walking through the dense jungle, they came to the small clearing where the temple was situated.  

“Casey, do you want to come into the temple with us?” 

Casey turned around. His dad was asking if he wanted to come with them! He didn’t know what to say. He knew that temples were scary, but he was really excited to see one that had never been explored before. “Sure,” he said, “I’d love to.” Little did he know that shortly after, he would regret his decision
