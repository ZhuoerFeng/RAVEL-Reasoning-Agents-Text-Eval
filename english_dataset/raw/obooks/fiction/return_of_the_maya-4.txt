“Casey? Casey!” shouted James as he searched for his missing son. “We found the way out! We’re going to come back with a search group and find you. I promise! All right Roberto, let’s go.”

Roberto looked at James and realized how heartbroken he was. They had been searching for over an hour when Roberto stumbled upon the stairs leading back up to the temple. After he found James, they had looked for Casey, but to no avail. Finally, they had given up, deciding that they’d come back tomorrow. Knowing these temples, he hoped Casey could survive until tomorrow...

As the world slowly came into focus through half-closed eyes, Casey sat up. *Where am I?* he thought. He put a hand to his head and felt a huge bump. Then it all came back to him. He must have fallen through some kind of trap door and hit his head.

By now his eyes had adjusted to the darkness, and he looked around for his flashlight. He couldn’t find it and figured that he must have dropped it outside the room. Before long, his eyes fell on a large, circular disk that was inlaid into the floor. It was roughly three meters across. The more Casey looked at it, the more he could make out.

Now that he looked more closely, he could see little glowing symbols. *Maybe some sort of optical illusion*, he thought. He moved over and put his hand to the floor. Unexpectedly, the strange disk started to spin, and he quickly took his hand away. He realized that it must be some kind of puzzle. Maybe it would open the door, so he could get out!

That was when he recognized the symbols. He’d seen them before. They were on the Maya calendar, predicting the end of the world. He slowly turned the symbols into place. Jaguar, Sun... He kept turning the circles that were inside of the larger circle. Then when the center disc was the only one left, he pushed it down and heard a small click. To his surprise, the whole disc slowly descended into the floor, and he realized that he had just made everything much worse.
