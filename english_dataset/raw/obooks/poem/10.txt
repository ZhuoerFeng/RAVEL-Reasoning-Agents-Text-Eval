## For All the Lovers

For all the lovers that ever were
I read this poem
For all those who ever saw dreams
In another’s eyes
I say these words

Now it seems I spent my life
Pretending to be me
For too many years
I fooled only those
Who don’t know me

When eyes meet eyes
And hand grips hand
A world begins to tumble
And now it’s downstream
All the way
All the way to heaven.
