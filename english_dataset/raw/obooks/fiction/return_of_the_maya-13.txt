*BEEP! BEEP! BEEP!* Casey jumped from his bed confused. He was... at his house? What about all the Maya and the battle? Relief teased his frayed emotions. It must have all been a dream! He had to tell his dad about this.

He rushed down the stairs calling out his father’s name and was so excited that he almost ran over his grandmother.

“Grandma! What are you doing here? This day just keeps getting better and better!”

“Casey? Oh my goodness, is that really you? How is this possible? Everyone thought you died in the battle, alongside your father.”

Alongside his father? Casey frowned. That could only mean one thing.

“You mean that Dad is...?” he cried out in exasperation.

“James is dead Casey. Everyone thought that you were as well, because you were gone for six years. You must...”

Casey knew that his grandma was still talking, but he didn’t hear her. He had stopped listening when he heard about his dad. Overcome with grief and anger, he pushed past his grandma and ran out of the door as fast as he could. He didn’t stop until he was completely lost. For the first time, he took in his surroundings. Where there used to be towering skyscrapers, there were now only the old husks of them. Many had holes in them and looked in danger of toppling over. This was all wrong. This was not the way it should end. Casey felt it in his bones. He knelt on the scorched earth and put his head in his hands. It was at this time that he realized that his hand was still clenched around the orb.

He stared at it dumbly, but the more he stared, the more he pondered. Then, it was as if he had been run over by a train. Finally, he knew the answer. The orb must have teleported him forward through time, which meant that it could probably teleport him backward as well. Casey knew he couldn’t live with himself if he didn’t at least try, and so, with no further thought, he twisted the orb on another random set of lines. Once again a portal opened up, and in the blink of an eye Casey was gone.
