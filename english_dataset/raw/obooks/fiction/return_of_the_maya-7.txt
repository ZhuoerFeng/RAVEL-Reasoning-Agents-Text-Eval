Casey was in the middle of telling James all about his adventures, when another excavation crew member entered. “Sir, we found the other part of the calendar,” then, seeing Casey, he said a little awkwardly, “Oh, I didn’t mean to intrude or anything, sir. Welcome back kid.” With that, he turned on his heel and was gone.

“I’ll talk to the excavation crew about uncovering this Maya Underground, but right now, I need to go see this calendar.” He turned away to leave, but then turned back, looked Casey in the eyes, and said, “I’m so glad you’re back, Kiddo.” Then he was gone.

Casey went over to his bed and curled up in the blanket. It felt coarse against his grazed knees. Slowly, he fell into an unsure sleep.

Fire. It was all around him, pouring down on all sides. He had no sense of direction, only that he must go forward. He ran, and dodged the burning trees that fell in his path. He needed to escape. He had to escape. Then, he saw his father standing over a large disc. “Dad!” he shouted. James didn’t turn, but instead a huge block of burning stone fell in between him and his dad.

Then he felt a strange warmth in his pocket. He put his hand inside, and brought out the silver orb. Glowing lines covered its surface. He threw it into the fire, and the world exploded into shards of light.

Casey woke up in a cold sweat. *How am I alive?* he thought. Then he realized that he had been dreaming. He felt something warm in his pocket and took out the little silver orb. It was glowing. Suddenly, he jumped up and ran to go find his dad.
