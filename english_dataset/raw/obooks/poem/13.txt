## Time and Love

The measured heart
Of the planet beats
Time is a tide
That never retreats

On burning days
Or beneath the moon
This world turns
Too slow, too soon

I am a son
Of the very last day
For you I threw
It all away

You’re a daughter
Of solid rock
Immune to time
And the rule of clock

May our love be shameless
In wind and light
As we ride together
Against the night.
