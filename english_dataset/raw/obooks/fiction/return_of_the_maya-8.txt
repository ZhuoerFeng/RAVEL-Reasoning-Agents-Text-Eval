As the center piece to the calendar fell into place, there was a sound like thunder, and then it began to glow around the edges. James stepped closer to get a better look. The calendar depicted a scene of what appeared to be spaceships coming to Earth. James stood up from his crouched position and moved back from the calendar. “Roberto, call whoever is in command of the army, and tell them that aliens are coming to Earth.”

“Sir?” said Roberto.

“Do as I say!” shouted James. Just then, Casey ran in.

“Dad! I had a horrible dream, and I just know that something bad is going to hap..” He cut off mid-sentence when he noticed the glowing calendar. “Is that..?”

“Yes, son, that is the Maya calendar. The complete one, I should say. It shows spaceships coming down to Earth. But don’t worry, we’ve already warned the army.”

“You’re basing all this on a stupid Calendar?” exclaimed Casey. “What if it isn’t right? What if they’re not coming to attack us?”

“Casey, calm down. I know what I’m talking about. You just need rest. Go lay down.” Furiously, Casey stormed out. Later that day, the army rolled into camp.
